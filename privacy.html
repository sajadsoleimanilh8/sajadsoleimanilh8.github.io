Privacy Policy

Sajad YouTube Tools · Last updated 14 November 2026

Sajad YouTube Tools is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels listed on the home page. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for access to the owner's YouTube account:

  • channel and video information, and channel analytics,
  • comments on the owner's videos,
  • permission to upload videos and to edit video details on the owner's channels.

How the data is used

Only to show the owner information about their own channels and to carry out actions the owner asks for, such as uploading a video or updating a title. The data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Where the data is stored

Sign-in tokens and any data the tool reads are stored only on the owner's own computer. The tool has no server or database of its own.

Who the data is shared with

The owner uses the tool together with an AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic). When the owner asks the assistant about their channel, the data needed to answer is sent to that assistant. The data is not shared with anyone else.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's files from the owner's computer removes all stored data.

Contact

Through the YouTube channels listed on the home page.
